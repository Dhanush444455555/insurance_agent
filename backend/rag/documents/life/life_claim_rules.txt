[LIFE CLAIMS & BENEFICIARY RULES]
1. Contestability Period: Section 45 / 3-year incontestability clause applies. Policies active for more than 3 consecutive years cannot be repudiated on grounds of misstatement or non-disclosure.
2. Suicide Exclusion: Suicide during the first 12 months from policy inception or revival limits benefit payout to surrender value or accumulated premiums paid.
3. Death Claim Documentation: Requires certified death certificate, original policy document, hospital discharge summaries (if applicable), and identity verification of registered nominees.
4. Rider Benefits: Accidental Death Benefit (ADB) and Critical Illness (CI) riders require specific evidence reports (e.g., police FIR for accidents or pathology histopathology reports for critical illness).
